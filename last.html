Bidya · Pillar 03

Last

Design for longevity

A business is only real when it outlives its hype. Last is about building something durable — a business that can survive storms, scale gracefully, and withstand your absence.

What this pillar covers

Built to survive storms
and your absence.

  1. 01 Cashflow mastery and financial buffers
  2. 02 Founder burnout and business dependency
  3. 03 Internal controls and governance structures
  4. 04 Business resilience and crisis planning
  5. 05 Self-audit systems and health checks
  6. 06 Advisory circles and mentorship models
  7. 07 Cultural systems and values inside the business
Who this is for

Established business owners building
for the long term.

You've been running your business for a few years. Revenue is steady, but you're the reason it works — and that scares you. If you stepped away for a month, things would fall apart. Last is for business owners who want a business that runs without them being in every room, every call, every decision.
