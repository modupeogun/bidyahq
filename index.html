Why Bidya exists

50% of African SMEs die
in their first year.

The ideas are there. The customers are there. The hustle is definitely there.

What's missing is structure — the frameworks, professional support, and real guidance that turn a hustle into a business. The kind of help that's always been reserved for bigger companies with bigger budgets.

Bidya is here to change that.

48% of Nigeria's GDP comes from SMEs
84% of Africa's workforce employed by small businesses
50% don't survive past year one

The Bidya Groundwork

A 6-week program for African
businesses that refuse to die.

We select 10 to 15 SMEs each cohort and pair each one with 2 to 3 vetted professionals — accountants, brand strategists, operations consultants, and more.

Over 6 weeks, they work on what's actually broken. Not theory. Not worksheets. The actual work.
