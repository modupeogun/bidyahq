Why Bidya exists

African SMEs power the economy.
They deserve proper support.

Small businesses employ 84% of Africa's workforce and contribute nearly half of Nigeria's GDP. Yet most founders are figuring it out alone — without the frameworks, professional guidance, or community that bigger businesses take for granted.

Bidya changes that. We give founders the tools, knowledge, and connections to build something that actually grows.

48% of Nigeria's GDP comes from SMEs
84% of Africa's workforce employed by small businesses

How we help you grow

Three areas. Real support.

Everything we share — every resource, every conversation, every guide — maps to one of three areas that matter most for growing African businesses.

Build

Get your foundation right — registration, finances, brand, pricing, and legal structure. The stuff most businesses skip and later regret.

Scale

Put systems in place that hold up as you grow — hiring, operations, marketing, and sales processes that don't fall apart when you're not watching.

Last

Build a business that survives without you in every room. Cashflow management, governance, resilience planning, and the systems that keep things running.
