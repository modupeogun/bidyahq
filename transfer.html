Bidya · Pillar 04

Transfer

Build beyond yourself

If it all ends with you, it was never really a business. Transfer is about building something that can be handed over — to a team, a family member, a buyer, or a system.

What this pillar covers

What happens after you
step away.

  1. 01 Exit planning and founder offboarding
  2. 02 Business valuation and packaging for sale
  3. 03 Generational transfer and succession planning
  4. 04 Documenting IP, processes, and key relationships
  5. 05 Internal leadership development
  6. 06 Selling your business vs. handing it down
  7. 07 Emotional preparation for founder exits
Who this is for

Founders thinking about
what comes next.

You've built something real — maybe over 5, 10, even 20 years. But you can't do this forever, and right now there's no plan for what happens when you stop. Transfer is for founders who want their business to outlive them, whether that means selling, handing it to family, or building a team that can carry it forward.
