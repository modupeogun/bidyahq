Bidya · Pillar 01

Build

Fix the foundation

Before you scale anything, you need something solid underneath. Build is about getting the fundamentals right — the things that most small businesses skip and later regret.

What this pillar covers

The fundamentals,
done properly.

  1. 01 Business registration and legal structure
  2. 02 Brand identity and communication clarity
  3. 03 Financial foundations — bookkeeping, accounts, and separation
  4. 04 Pricing strategy and product-market clarity
  5. 05 First team hires — who, when, and how
  6. 06 Contracts, compliance, and NDAs
  7. 07 Decision-making frameworks for early-stage founders
Who this is for

Early-stage founders
getting serious.

You started something — maybe out of necessity, maybe out of vision. Either way, you're past the idea stage but the foundation underneath is shaky. You're making money but not managing it. You have customers but no real brand. You need structure before you can scale anything, and Build is where that starts.
