The 6-week structure

What happens inside
the Groundwork.

Week 1 — Diagnose

What is actually broken — not what they think is broken

What the SME does

Complete a business health audit. Map the current state of finances, ops, brand, and team honestly.

What the professionals do

Review the audit. Each professional identifies 2 to 3 root issues. No solutions yet — diagnosis only.

Deliverable

A shared problem map agreed between founder and professionals.

Week 2 — Prioritise

Not everything can be fixed — choose what matters most

What the SME does

Rank top 3 problems by impact and urgency. Share with professionals for a reality check.

What the professionals do

Challenge or validate the ranking. Agree on one primary focus area for weeks 3 to 5.

Deliverable

A clear 3-priority list with one agreed primary focus. Written and signed off.

Week 3 — Build

Do the actual work on priority one

What the SME does

Execute first assigned tasks — bookkeeping setup, repriced products, business registration, or brand clarity.

What the professionals do

Guide, review, and unblock. At least one live check-in session this week.

Deliverable

First tangible fix in place and documented.

Week 4 — Deepen

Tackle priority two and stress-test priority one

What the SME does

Begin work on second priority. Test whether Week 3 fix is holding up.

What the professionals do

Introduce additional tools or contacts. Flag if priority one needs revisiting.

Deliverable

Mid-cohort check-in call with Bidya team.

Week 5 — Systemise

Turn fixes into processes that run without the founder

What the SME does

Document every fix as a repeatable process. Write simple one-page SOPs for each key change.

What the professionals do

Review SOPs. Confirm what can now run without their involvement.

Deliverable

A simple operations playbook. The founder owns this permanently.

Week 6 — Close & continue

Celebrate what changed. Plan what comes next.

What the SME does

Present before and after to the full cohort. Share the Groundwork story.

What the professionals do

Write a final assessment note. Write a short endorsement for the founder's story.

Deliverable

The Groundwork Report — a case study published with founder permission.
